# Analysis of Guangdong Integrated Circuit Enterprises  
## I. Market Concentration Analysis (CR5/CR10)  
### 1.1 Core Concentration Indicators  
- CR5 (Market share of top 5 enterprises): 52.56%  
The total registered capital of the top 5 enterprises reaches 5,915.55 billion yuan, accounting for more than half of the industry’s total capital.  
  
- CR10 (Market share of top 10 enterprises): 67.15%  
The total registered capital of the top 10 enterprises stands at 7,556.72 billion yuan, controlling over two-thirds of the industry’s capital.  
  
- CR30 (Market share of top 30 enterprises): 88.70%  
The top 30 enterprises have nearly monopolized the capital resources of the industry.  
  
### 1.2 Market Structure Judgment  
Based on the CR10 indicator of 67.15%, the integrated circuit industry in Guangdong Province presents a high oligopolistic market structure:  
- Market dominance is highly concentrated in a small number of large enterprises.  
- Leading enterprises possess absolute competitive advantages in capital scale.  
- New entrants face extremely high capital barriers.  
  
## II. Regional Distribution Analysis  
### 2.1 Regional Distribution of Enterprise Quantity (Top 3)  
- Shenzhen: 5,849 enterprises (58.69%) – Absolute core city  
- Guangzhou: 1,633 enterprises (16.39%) – Second largest agglomeration  
- Dongguan: 966 enterprises (9.69%) – Key supporting base  
  
### 2.2 Regional Distribution of Registered Capital (Top 3)  
- Shenzhen: 7,866.72 billion yuan (69.90%) – Core capital agglomeration hub  
- Zhuhai: 2,139.56 billion yuan (19.01%) – Second largest capital scale  
- Jiangmen: 580.02 billion yuan (5.15%) – Important capital node  
  
### 2.3 Concentration of Core Pearl River Delta Cities  
- Enterprise quantity concentration: 95.64% (enterprises in 9 Pearl River Delta cities account for the total of Guangdong Province)  
- Registered capital concentration: 99.80% (capital of 9 Pearl River Delta cities accounts for the total of Guangdong Province)  
  
**Conclusion**: The integrated circuit industry of Guangdong Province is highly concentrated in the Pearl River Delta region, showing obvious regional agglomeration characteristics.  
  
## III. Enterprise Type Structure Analysis  
- Small and medium-sized enterprises: 9,060 enterprises (90.30%) – Dominant in quantity but small in capital scale  
- Industrial above-scale enterprises: 588 enterprises (5.86%) – Medium-sized enterprises serving as industrial support  
- Leading enterprises: 385 enterprises (3.84%) – The smallest in quantity but control the major capital resources
