**广东 2026 年政府工作报告：构建现代化产业体系**

广东省2026年政府工作报告与高质量发展相关部署，明确构建“**传统产业筑基、新兴产业领航、未来产业布局**”的现代化产业体系，形成三类产业协同融通的发展格局。以下是重点发展与未来重点布局的产业全景：

**一、传统产业（优化升级，打造“永不落幕”集群）**

政府工作报告明确提出，广东传统产业占规模以上制造业增加值比重超七成，是制造业当家的基本盘：

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 重点领域 | 升级方向 | 目标 |
| 电子、机械、化工、轻工、建材、纺织、冶金、矿业、船舶 | 数字化、智能化、高端化转型 | 存量升级，提升全球分工地位 |
| 家电、食品、服装、家具、珠宝、玩具、制鞋 | 厚植品牌优势，推广数智化“链式改造” | 打造强大的“永不落幕”传统产业集群 |

核心举措：实施“人工智能+制造”专项行动，推动传统产业全链条升级，单个工人效率提升可达25%以上。

**二、新兴产业（壮大支柱，锻造万亿级集群）**

政府工作报告列出九大重点新兴产业，作为2026年发展核心：

1. **新能源**：包括新能源汽车、新型储能、海上风电等，比亚迪、广汽埃安等项目总投资超520亿元
2. **新材料**：突破半导体材料、高端合金材料等关键领域，为先进制造提供支撑
3. **智能网联汽车**：打造完整产业链，推动自动驾驶技术商业化应用
4. **智能机器人**：广东工业机器人产量连续多年居全国第一，全国每3台就有1台“广东造”
5. **医药和医疗器械**：布局珠海大健康、中山医疗器械产业园，总投资190亿元
6. **航空航天**：发展商业航天、卫星应用等领域
7. **集成电路**：推进深圳中芯国际12英寸芯片生产线（800亿元）、广州粤芯半导体三期（360亿元）等项目，夯实“广东强芯”工程
8. **低空经济**：广东坐拥全国超30%的产业链企业，在全球无人机市场占据重要地位
9. **生物制造**：发展合成生物学、生物基材料等前沿领域

核心目标：一体推进创新设施建设、技术研发、产品迭代，培育更多新兴支柱产业，锻造更多千亿元级、万亿元级新兴产业集群。

**三、未来产业（前瞻布局，抢占新赛道）**

政府工作报告明确七大未来产业重点细分领域，面向“十五五”规划：

1. **第六代移动通信（6G）**：布局网络技术研发与基础设施建设
2. **具身智能**：2026年被视为从“0”到“1”跨越的关键窗口期，广东企业在CES展表现突出
3. **细胞与基因治疗**：打造深圳光明科学城、广州生物岛创新集群，总投资420亿元
4. **脑科学与脑机接口**：抢占生命科学前沿高地
5. **氢能及先进核能**：构建未来能源体系
6. **深海探测**：发展海洋经济与深海开发项目，总投资680亿元
7. **量子科技**：布局量子计算、量子通信等领域

核心策略：按照“集中力量办大事”原则，重点保障人工智能终端、具身智能机器人、固态电池等新赛道产业和项目，分行业、分领域、分批次更新发布新赛道指引。

**四、高质量发展报告补充重点**

《2026广东省高质量发展态势与“十五五”战略前瞻分析报告》进一步明确三大方向：

1. **产业焕新**：全面拥抱“人工智能+”，推动家电、服装等传统产业全链条AI赋能，实现从研发设计到全球营销的深度转型
2. **动能转换**：强化鹏城实验室、广州实验室等平台作用，打通科技成果转化“最后一公里”，发展耐心资本与科技金融
3. **空间拓展**：布局53个新赛道（围绕智能链接、生物融合、先进制造、空间拓展、未来能源、品质生活六大领域），力争5年内培育形成3个以上万亿级赛道

**五、产业发展总体格局**

广东构建现代化产业体系遵循“三重奏”逻辑：

1. 传统产业“筑基”：占规模以上制造业增加值比重超七成，是基本盘
2. 新兴产业“顶梁”：九大领域加速成长，已形成超高清视频等10个万亿级产业集群
3. 未来产业“领航”：七大前沿领域提前布局，为“十五五”期间产业升级储备新动能

核心战略：坚持实体经济为本、制造业当家，加快构建以先进制造业为骨干、具有广东特点和广东优势、具有强大国际竞争力的现代化产业体系。

**Guangdong 2026 Government Work Report: Building a Modern Industrial System**

The Guangdong Provincial 2026 Government Work Report outlines key deployments for high-quality development, explicitly defining a modern industrial system characterized by **"traditional industries as the foundation, emerging industries as the leaders, and future industries as the strategic layout."** This structure aims to create a synergistic development pattern across three tiers of industry. Below is the comprehensive industrial landscape of key developments and future layouts:

### I. Traditional Industries (Optimization and Upgrading: Creating "Evergreen" Clusters)

The report clarifies that traditional industries account for over **70%** of the value-added of manufacturing enterprises above designated size, forming the bedrock of the "Manufacturing as the Mainstay" strategy.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Key Fields** | **Upgrade Direction** | **Objectives** |
| Electronics, Machinery, Chemicals, Light Industry, Building Materials, Textiles, Metallurgy, Mining, Shipbuilding | Digital, intelligent, and high-end transformation | Stock optimization; elevating position in the global division of labor |
| Home Appliances, Food, Apparel, Furniture, Jewelry, Toys, Footwear | Strengthening brand advantages; promoting "chain-based" digital transformation | Cultivating powerful and "ever-lasting" traditional industrial clusters |

* **Core Initiative:** Implementation of the **"Artificial Intelligence + Manufacturing"** special action plan to drive full-chain upgrades. This aims to increase individual worker productivity by over **25%**.

### II. Emerging Industries (Strengthening Pillars: Forging Trillion-Yuan Clusters)

The report identifies nine priority emerging industries as the core drivers for 2026:

1. **New Energy:** Including NEVs, new energy storage, and offshore wind power. Total investment in projects like BYD and GAC Aion exceeds **52 billion RMB**.
2. **New Materials:** Breakthroughs in semiconductor materials and high-end alloys to support advanced manufacturing.
3. **Intelligent Connected Vehicles (ICV):** Building a complete supply chain and promoting the commercial application of autonomous driving technology.
4. **Intelligent Robotics:** Guangdong’s industrial robot output has ranked first in China for years; **one out of every three** robots in China is "Made in Guangdong."
5. **Pharmaceuticals & Medical Devices:** Development of the Zhuhai Big Health and Zhongshan Medical Device industrial parks, with a total investment of **19 billion RMB**.
6. **Aerospace:** Development of commercial spaceflight and satellite applications.
7. **Integrated Circuits (IC):** Advancing the **80-billion RMB** SMIC 12-inch chip line in Shenzhen and the **36-billion RMB** Yuexin Semiconductor Phase III in Guangzhou to solidify the "Guangdong Strong Chip" project.
8. **Low-Altitude Economy:** Guangdong hosts over **30%** of China’s supply chain enterprises and holds a dominant position in the global drone market.
9. **Biomanufacturing:** Development of synthetic biology, bio-based materials, and other frontier fields.

* **Core Goal:** Simultaneously advance innovation facilities, R&D, and product iteration to cultivate more pillar industries and forge additional **100-billion** and **trillion-yuan** emerging industrial clusters.

### III. Future Industries (Forward-Looking Layout: Seizing New Tracks)

The report specifies seven priority sub-sectors for future industries, targeting the **"15th Five-Year Plan"** (2026–2030):

1. **6G (Sixth-Generation Mobile Communications):** R&D in network technology and infrastructure layout.
2. **Embodied AI:** 2026 is viewed as the critical window for the "0 to 1" breakthrough; Guangdong enterprises showed prominent performance at CES.
3. **Cell and Gene Therapy:** Creating innovation clusters in Shenzhen Guangming Science City and Guangzhou Bio-island, with a total investment of **42 billion RMB**.
4. **Brain Science and Brain-Computer Interface (BCI):** Capturing the frontiers of life sciences.
5. **Hydrogen & Advanced Nuclear Energy:** Constructing a future energy system.
6. **Deep-Sea Exploration:** Developing the marine economy and deep-sea exploration projects, with a total investment of **68 billion RMB**.
7. **Quantum Technology:** Layouts in quantum computing and quantum communications.

* **Core Strategy:** Adhering to the principle of **"concentrating resources on major tasks,"** the province will prioritize support for new tracks such as AI terminals, embodied robots, and solid-state batteries, releasing "New Track Guidelines" by industry and sector in batches.

### IV. High-Quality Development Report: Key Supplements

The *2026 Guangdong High-Quality Development Trend and "15th Five-Year" Strategic Analysis Report* further defines three directions:

1. **Industrial Renewal:** Fully embracing **"AI+"** to empower traditional sectors like appliances and apparel across the entire chain—from R&D and design to global marketing.
2. **Kinetic Energy Conversion:** Strengthening the role of platforms like Peng Cheng Laboratory and Guangzhou Laboratory to bridge the "last mile" of scientific achievement commercialization. Developing **"patient capital"** and technology finance.
3. **Spatial Expansion:** Mapping out **53 new tracks** (around six domains: intelligent links, bio-fusion, advanced manufacturing, spatial expansion, future energy, and quality life), aiming to cultivate **3+ trillion-yuan tracks** within five years.

### V. Overall Industrial Development Pattern

Guangdong's construction of a modern industrial system follows a **"Trilogy"** logic:

1. **Traditional Industries "The Foundation":** Accounting for 70%+ of the manufacturing base; they remain the bedrock.
2. **Emerging Industries "The Pillars":** Nine major fields are accelerating; **10 trillion-yuan clusters** (e.g., Ultra-HD Video) have already been formed.
3. **Future Industries "The Pilot":** Seven frontier fields are being strategically positioned to reserve new momentum for industrial upgrades during the "15th Five-Year Plan."

* **Core Strategy:** Persisting in the **"Entity Economy as the Origin"** and **"Manufacturing as the Mainstay."** Accelerating the construction of a modern industrial system with advanced manufacturing as the backbone, possessing unique Guangdong advantages and strong international competitiveness.
